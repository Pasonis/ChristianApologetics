# free online reference points

* **New Advent — Fathers of the Church archive**
  * Good for quickly checking whether a work is available free online.
  * Often breaks works into author pages, work pages, and sometimes book-level pages.
  * Useful searches:
    * `site:newadvent.org/fathers Chrysostom Homily 14 on the Statues`
    * `site:newadvent.org/fathers Ambrose On the Duties of the Clergy Book III`
    * `site:newadvent.org/fathers John of Damascus Orthodox Faith`

* **CCEL — Early Church Fathers / NPNF volume pages**
  * Good for checking exact volume contents and table of contents structure.
  * Useful when the Kindle metadata is vague or uses alternate names.
  * Useful searches:
    * `site:ccel.org npnf109`
    * `site:ccel.org npnf114`
    * `site:ccel.org npnf209`

# title-alias map

* **Homilies on the Statues** = sometimes surfaced as **Homilies on the Statutes**
* **Treatise Concerning the Christian Priesthood** = **On the Priesthood**
* **An Exact Exposition of the Orthodox Faith** = **Exposition of the Orthodox Faith** = **On the Orthodox Faith**
* **The City of God** = **De Civitate Dei**
* **On Christian Doctrine** = **De Doctrina Christiana**
* **On the Holy Trinity** = **De Trinitate**
  * could be Augustine or Hilary, so include the author in searches
* **On the Incarnation of the Word** = **On the Incarnation** = **De Incarnatione**
* **Three Treatises on the Divine Images** = **Three Apologies Against Those Who Attack / Decry the Holy Images**
* **Questions on the Octateuch** = **Quaestiones in Octateuchum**
* **The Triads** = **Triads in Defense of the Holy Hesychasts**
