## Best reading path for your exact question

I would read them in this order:

1. **Gregory Nazianzen, Letter 101 to Cledonius**
   for the principle: full assumption of true humanity is necessary for full healing. ([New Advent][2])

2. **John of Damascus, Exposition of the Orthodox Faith, Book III**
   for the clean distinction between:

   * what Christ really assumed
   * the natural/blameless passions
   * and the fact that these did not dominate His will ([New Advent][1])
