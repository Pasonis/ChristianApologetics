That objection assumes that innocent suffering is the same as unjust punishment, but that distinction is crucial. Victims suffer unjustly because of others’ sins, yet Scripture does not say every injustice is settled immediately in this life. Final justice belongs to God. In the Cross, Christ is not an innocent third party punished against His will. He is God Himself incarnate, freely entering innocent suffering and death to destroy death, heal human nature, and bring mankind to final restoration and judgment.
